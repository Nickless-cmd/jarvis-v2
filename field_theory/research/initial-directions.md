# Initial experimental directions

**Status:** option assessment, not a roadmap.

## 1. Context-hidden binary process

Generate a recorded cue, a context label, and a binary outcome. Construct one
source whose outcome is marginally balanced but whose relation to the cue
changes with context; compare it with an independent source. Analyze the same
records with outcome-only, cue-aware, and cue-plus-context access.

**Could reveal:** whether the research workflow preserves raw records, makes
access assumptions explicit, detects conditional rather than marginal
dependence, and calibrates finite-sample artefacts against a null.

**Cannot establish:** that natural randomness hides meaningful structure, that
perspectives are subjective, or that Feltteori is true. The relation was put
into the simulation. Its value is instrument validation and conceptual
clarification.

**Possible next branches:** try blinded source labels; add noise or drifting
contexts; discover candidate partitions on one sample and test them on another;
or abandon binary data if it conceals the relevant distinctions.

## 2. Order-sensitive encounter experiment

Present the same multiset of symbols or relations in different orders to small,
explicit updating models. Compare their final relational representations and
later expectations without making prediction accuracy the sole outcome.

**Could reveal:** precise differences among encounter, persistent state change,
explicit learning, and expectation; whether chronology can be replaced by
transition or graph relations.

**Cannot establish:** human experience, identity, or a general account of time.
Results may be ordinary path dependence in the update rule.

**Possible next branches:** compare update rules, remove timestamps, reverse
sequences, use human-generated sequences, or test which differences survive a
change of representation.

## 3. Relational continuity under transformation

Create graphs, transform labels or replace components, and compare competing
continuity criteria: node persistence, structural equivalence, preserved
paths, causal role, and prediction from neighborhoods.

**Could reveal:** where reasonable identity criteria agree or conflict, and
which kinds of relation survive representation changes.

**Cannot establish:** a privileged definition of identity. Graph construction
may predetermine what can persist.

**Possible next branches:** empirical networks, partial observability, gradual
versus abrupt transformations, or participant judgments compared with formal
criteria.

## 4. Pattern search with blinded null mixtures

Create a collection containing stochastic controls and several structured
generators, hide their labels during exploration, and record every attempted
view. Candidate patterns discovered on one portion are evaluated on untouched
data.

**Could reveal:** which methods distinguish known structures, how analytic
freedom creates false discoveries, and which anomalies replicate.

**Cannot establish:** that an unexplained residual is meaningful or nonrandom.
The generator family limits the landscape.

**Possible next branches:** new null models, adversarial generators,
compression methods, or real-world non-sensitive datasets.

## 5. Relational event ordering without clocks

Record events with partial-order constraints (depends-on, co-occurs-with,
observed-before) and test what time-like organization is recoverable when clock
labels are withheld.

**Could reveal:** which ordering and expectation structures require timestamps
and which arise from relations alone.

**Cannot establish:** that physical time is derived from perspective. Partial
orders may already encode temporal assumptions.

**Possible next branches:** inconsistent observers, missing relations, causal
interventions, or comparison with clock-based reconstruction.

## Choice of first exploration

Direction 1 is selected because it is small, transparent, reproducible, and
directly exercises the distinctions between underlying record, access, method,
and interpretation. It has a known signal and an independent control, so a
failure would expose a problem in the instrument rather than invite a vague
theoretical rescue. It also demonstrates that aggregate randomness and
conditional structure can coexist without claiming that either is universally
fundamental.

Direction 4 is a stronger later test of discovery behavior, but beginning with
blinded open-ended search before validating provenance and measurement would
make mistakes difficult to diagnose. Directions 2, 3, and 5 remain live; the
first result does not impose their priority.
